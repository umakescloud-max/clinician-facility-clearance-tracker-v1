Your contract clinician is cleared at one facility and blocked at the next, and you only find out from an email you read a day late.

I built a clearance tracker for small groups that send speech-language pathologists into hospitals and nursing facilities.

Every clinician-facility packet sits in one list. Each row shows the next missing item and the days left until the first scheduled study.

When a facility returns a packet, it lands on a Blocked screen with the reason named. In the demo, a liability certificate expires in 12 days, but the facility needs coverage 30 days past the start date. The first study is in 4 days, and a countdown shows it.

The coordinator attaches the renewed certificate, reads the drafted cover note and clicks send. Nothing goes to a facility on its own.
